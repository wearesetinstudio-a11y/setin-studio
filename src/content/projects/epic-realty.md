  - label: Research & Brand Sprint
    title: Uncovering Visual Clichés in a Template-Driven Market
    text: We kicked off the project with an intensive Brand Sprint workshop alongside Epic Realty’s founders. Using Simon Sinek’s Golden Circle framework, we defined the company’s core mission around elevating luxury real estate standards in Panama through genuine relationships, absolute discretion, and architectural expertise. This process allowed us to shape the brand personality as a thoughtful blend of maturity, elegance, strategic insight, and a touch of boldness, laying a strong foundation to reject impersonal sales tactics. To validate this direction, we conducted a thorough competitive audit that revealed a local market dominated by generic templates. On top of that, Epic’s legacy WordPress site suffered from structural chaos, making listing management frustrating and significantly weakening their high-end market positioning.
    afterGroup: 1
  - label: Brand Identity Challenge
    title: From Legibility Issues to an Adaptive Ecosystem
    text: The weaknesses identified during the audit extended directly to the visual identity, where the legacy logo posed severe operational challenges. Its ultra-thin lines and awkward proportions made it fall apart at smaller scales, while the muted color scheme felt completely out of touch with a luxury positioning. In response to these challenges, we built a flexible visual ecosystem that establishes a commanding presence across all touchpoints. This proved especially crucial in the Panamanian market, where social profiles and messaging apps act as the primary point of contact, making a sharp social avatar and icon just as vital as high-end printed brochures or real estate catalogs.
    afterGroup: 2
  - label: Visual Direction
    title: Editorial Brutalism Meets Timeless Sophistication
    text: Building directly on this refined identity, we made intentional choices in typography and color to distinctly set Epic Realty apart from its peers. Instead of defaulting to traditional luxury serifs, we chose Barlow Condensed for its bold architectural character, balancing it with Lato for clean and effortless body copy. The color strategy draws inspiration from Panama’s lush landscape and understated elegance. Rich tones of Woodland Green and Panama Night provide a grounded foundation, complemented by subtle Boutique Green accents and generous white space to deliver a clean, elevated feel.
    afterGroup: 3
  - label: UX & UI Strategy
    title: Immersive Imagery Grounded in Clear Technical Metrics
    text: This focus on visual clarity guided the design of the website’s user interface, where high-resolution imagery and video walkthroughs take center stage. We structured the property listing pages to seamlessly guide users from rich editorial storytelling on the left to an organized technical spec panel on the right. To further reinforce Epic's standing as a trusted market authority, we introduced a custom Valor EPIC en la zona module, which directly benchmarks a property's price per square meter against the neighborhood average.
    afterGroup: 4
  - label: Platform Architecture
    title: Seamless Content Management Built for Growth
    text: To support this thoughtful interface, we migrated the website from an outdated WordPress environment over to Webflow. We engineered a tailored CMS architecture featuring dedicated database collections for properties and market insights. This grants the founders complete autonomy to publish and edit listings smoothly without risking design integrity. Additionally, the underlying system was structured to scale effortlessly, allowing for future expansions like advanced multi-filtering and dedicated neighborhood landing pages.
    afterGroup: 5
  - label: Conversion Strategy
    title: Streamlined Inquiries & Data-Driven Optimization
    text: The final piece of the strategy focused on building a frictionless conversion pathway that seamlessly turns casual browsing into active inquiries. We designed the main call-to-action to open a contextual contact modal linked directly to the specific property being viewed. Recognizing that WhatsApp serves as the primary communication hub in Panama, we integrated a direct chat trigger right into the main navigation. The entire page architecture was engineered to support ongoing analytics, paving the way for straightforward A/B testing and continuous conversion rate optimization.
    afterGroup: 6
  - label: Results
    title: A Solid Foundation for Panama's Premier Property Brand
    text: The Epic Realty project successfully bridges high-end branding with modern web architecture. Replacing generic industry templates with Barlow Condensed typography, a sophisticated color strategy, and clear information hierarchy positions Epic to command market authority. The platform provides a scalable foundation that empowers the brand to set new standards in luxury real estate.
