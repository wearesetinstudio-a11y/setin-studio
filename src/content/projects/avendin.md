overviewText: |-
  Panama’s snack and beverage distribution relied on traditional retail setups for years. Recognizing an emerging gap, the founders of Snacks Lovers Pty and Mexi Munchies established Avendin to import high-tech vending hardware directly from the USA and Asia. Starting from a blank slate, the business required a positioning system that would assert its technical capability and secure immediate authority in the region.

  Analyzing their commercial model revealed a distinct communication challenge. While end-consumers buy the items inside the machine, the primary business decision-makers are facility managers, shopping center owners, real estate developers, and public administrators. Rather than a loud consumer graphic layer, Avendin needed a restrained and business-focused visual identity that builds institutional trust during B2B lease negotiations.

  - label: 'Research'
    title: 'Defining the B2B Target Audience Through Focused Workshops'
    text: |-
      Due to the absence of baseline market research, we initiated the project with stakeholder interviews and an intensive brand sprint alongside the founders. As a compact studio focused on commercial logic, we mapped out the operational requirements of commercial real estate directors and public institution managers. Visual moodboards and competitive auditing established the strategic boundaries for the new identity.

      Building on these findings, we determined that the brand framework had to communicate technological sophistication, reliability, and modern engineering. Instead of generic decorative graphics, the system centers on operational facts: zero upfront investment ($0.00 USD) for property managers, full maintenance coverage, and TCN High-Tech machine hardware. This direction positioned Avendin as an infrastructure partner rather than a simple machine operator.
    afterGroup: 2
  - label: 'Mark'
    title: 'Custom Typography Designed for Technical Precision'
    text: |-
      Vending operators routinely rely on repetitive snack or cup illustrations. We bypassed these visual tropes to focus on the typographic potential of the name AVENDIN. We centered our creative exploration on structural typography, aiming to construct a clean mark with an industrial edge.

      Translating this concept into letterforms, we implemented a calculated mix of uppercase and lowercase shapes. The characters A, V, D, and I use uppercase proportions, while E and N feature lowercase geometry. This balance gave us exact control over letter spacing and internal counter-forms. To solidify the technical character, we applied 65-degree diagonal cuts to the letters A and E, providing a dynamic visual signature while maintaining high legibility.
    afterGroup: 3
  - label: 'System'
    title: 'High-Contrast Palette and Digital Typographic Layouts'
    text: |-
      Building a complete visual system required tools that reinforce high-tech capability. We specified Instrument Sans as the primary typeface due to its structural clarity across digital displays, technical spec sheets, and printed pitch decks.

      The color system relies on deep background tones—Deep Nebula (#0D002E) and Urban Graphite (#373340)—to establish a solid technical foundation. These are contrasted with Signal Violet (#8B64FF) and Pure Light (#FFFFFF) to highlight key performance metrics. The resulting color hierarchy projects transparency and clarity, ensuring B2B proposals remain easy to digest for corporate clients.
    afterGroup: 4
  - label: 'Results'
    title: 'Market Entry Success and Stronger Corporate Trust'
    text: |-
      Translating business goals into a structured brand identity enabled Avendin to enter the market as an established infrastructure provider. Removing graphic clutter and focusing on technical reliability helped the company open doors to high-tier commercial locations.

      With clear offer structures and a cohesive digital presence, Avendin shortened negotiation cycles with property owners. The brand successfully built market authority in Panama by bridging high-tech vending equipment with straightforward business transparency.
