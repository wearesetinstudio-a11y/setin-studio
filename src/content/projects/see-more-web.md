    text: |-
      Market research and a competitive audit of industry alternatives defined our starting point for shaping a unique value proposition. Since See More Logistics had no prior web presence, the research phase centered on benchmarking market standards and identifying key communication gaps.
      
      The analysis revealed a strong demand for alternatives to impersonal, global logistics corporations. These legacy entities often reduce client service to rigid automated portals and heavy paperwork. In response, we positioned See More Logistics as an agile, innovative partner operating with a boutique service mindset.
      
      The updated communication strategy highlights personalized business relationships, dedicated account teams, and high-touch service quality. The site demonstrates that the company combines a modern tech-startup aesthetic with the operational reliability essential to the B2B sector.

  - label: "UX/UI design"
    title: "UX Architecture and UI Aesthetics"
    text: |-
      The interface structure caters specifically to the Panamanian market, where See More Logistics concentrates its current operations. The core UX objective was to create a concise, highly readable landing page that immediately establishes credibility and defines the brand’s regional presence.
      
      The hero section features a visual anchor tied to the brand's core identity. In the background, a simplified architectural silhouette of Panama merges with an animated moving truck. This subtle kinetic touch communicates the core business and geographic scope within seconds. An animated logo preloader introduces the experience, reinforcing a professional tone from the moment the site loads.
      
      Navigation relies on a persistent header bar that provides continuous access to all page sections. Content flow guides the visitor from brand definitions through service details and operational methodology to conversion points. Given B2B requirements, establishing trust through social proof was paramount: client testimonials, partner logos, and project experience with the Panamanian government occupy prominent positions. A clean, direct contact form closes the journey.
      
      For the visual layer, we implemented a restrained, European-inspired minimalist aesthetic requested by the client. Clean layouts, strong typographic hierarchy, and a refined UI kit met executive expectations, leading to immediate approval of the artistic direction.

  - label: "Engineering"
    title: "Technology and Motion Design Layer"
    text: |-
      Selecting an Astro-based architecture paired with Cursor allowed us to complete full frontend development within two business days. This technical efficiency shifted project dynamics: rather than managing structural bottlenecks, we devoted total operational time to refining interaction details, performance, and visual polish. The outcome is a lightweight, ultra-fast application that handles rich animations without performance drops.
      
      To elevate brand engagement, we deployed three complementary animation technologies across the interface:
      
      The primary visual landscape—a minimalist Panama skyline with a moving vehicle—was built using Rive. This ensures full scalability across mobile and desktop devices with minimal file weight. The vector loop naturally engages visitors and grounds the brand in its operational territory.
      
      Service offerings and the logo preloader use Lottie files exported directly from Figma assets. The preloader establishes high quality upon entry, while service loops maintain visual interest across the offer grid.
      
      Page borders and section dividers double as a virtual road network where custom geometric vehicles move across the screen. We added contextual state logic: when a vehicle enters a dark section background, its headlights switch on automatically. This handcrafted detail brings the page to life and reflects meticulous engineering standards.
