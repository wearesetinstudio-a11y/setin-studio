overviewTitle: 'Rebranding Znicz Pruszków to resolve visual inefficiencies in domestic football'
overviewText: |-
  Znicz Pruszków carries a long tradition of developing football talent that eventually reached the highest international levels. However, the club’s sporting standard in the Polish First League was held back by an outdated visual identity. The previous crest suffered from color inconsistencies, poor digital scalability, and obsolete typography. Consequently, the club underutilized its commercial and community potential. We initiated an unofficial rebranding concept to demonstrate how a traditional football club can transition into a modern sports brand.

sections:
  - label: Strategy
    title: Visual identity as a direct commercial asset and growth engine
    text: |-
      Sports market benchmarks confirm that a coherent visual identity operates directly as a commercial asset. A structured brand experience drives merchandise sales, strengthens fan retention, and builds credibility with prospective sponsors. Our strategy focused on maintaining historical continuity while engineering a resilient identity system built for digital touchpoints and broadcast media. We aimed to pair pragmatic brand architecture with a clean, confident aesthetic.
    afterGroup: 1
  - label: Symbol
    title: A geometric distillation of the club flame enclosed in a circular crest
    text: |-
      The redesigned crest distills the core flame motif into a precise geometric form. Enclosed within a circular shield, the composition references established European football standards. We overhauled the typographic layout by replacing legacy lettering with a sharp sans-serif structure, integrating the MKS acronym, the full club name, and the founding year 1923. The resulting symbol scales seamlessly across digital interfaces and physical applications, supported by simplified secondary marks for compact formats.
    afterGroup: 2
  - label: System
    title: Refined historic palette paired with high-contrast editorial typography
    text: |-
      The brand architecture refines the club's historic color palette. We replaced the oversaturated yellow with a deep, balanced tone inspired by natural fire, paired with rich burgundy and neutral black. The typographic hierarchy pairs Pirata One for high-contrast headline statements with Farro and Lato for structured editorial layouts and digital user interfaces. This combination maintains clear readability while preserving a distinct expressive character.
    afterGroup: 3
  - label: Implementation
    title: Production-ready kit designs leveraging realistic textile simulations
    text: |-
      The concept extends into full match kits engineered for production. The home kit features a retro-inspired cream and red palette with vertical striping and internal collar detailing. The away kit delivers a stark, modern dark contrast. Using advanced rendering tools, we articulated fabric textures, stitching, and material finish under match conditions. The completed proposal has been submitted to the board of Znicz Pruszków as a turn-key brand transformation model.

La estrategia se centró en respetar la continuidad histórica mientras optimizábamos la arquitectura del signo para entornos digitales y soportes físicos, combinando pragmatismo comercial con una estética limpia y decidida.
