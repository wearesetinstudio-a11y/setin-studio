overviewText: |-
  For us as designers based in Szczecin, Bar Turysta represents far more than a standard dining venue or a routine point on the urban map. It stands as an authentic city icon, operating continuously since 1964 and forming an indispensable part of our local collective memory. We remember this place from childhood, when visiting the canteen with our parents for Friday dinners or homemade fruit shakes after school meant a genuine ritual. Our parents' advice to avoid modern fast food in favor of an honest meal at Turysta shaped our early appreciation for culinary integrity and unpretentious quality. In local dialect, no one speaks of a generic milk bar; everyone simply refers to the destination by its proper name.
  
  This internal conceptual project stems from a deep sense of pride and gratitude toward a venue that forged a fragment of our design identity. With every creative decision, we challenged ourselves to determine whether our solution honored the raw spirit of the era or merely surrendered to an artificial retro styling. We aimed to capture the core of Turysta as a simple, imperfect, yet profoundly honest space built by ordinary people for ordinary people. Consequently, this initiative bridges historical gravity with a balanced contemporary branding execution.

  title: 'Controlled evolution rejecting superficial pastiche and forced trends'
  text: |-
    The foundation of our strategy rests on controlled evolution rather than aggressive modernization. The core strength of Bar Turysta does not lie in forced trendiness or conversion into a commercial venue, but in its uncompromising authenticity. We established the historical ethos of Szczecin shipyard workers—who sought a hearty, affordable meal among peers after a demanding shift—as our primary strategic benchmark. We honor this heritage without locking the brand inside a stagnant museum exhibit.
    
    Simultaneously, we aim to encourage the younger generation of Szczecin residents to recognize the unique cultural and social value of sharing a traditional meal around a common table. Although the Setin Studio team matured in post-communist Poland, we thoroughly understand and respect this tradition through family narratives. Precise brand positioning enables us to speak effectively to long-time patrons as well as students and travelers seeking genuine local experiences. We translate localized nostalgia into a living, strategic asset of our studio.

  text: |-
    The previous building facade failed to utilize its full potential and remained largely illegible to casual passersby on the street. This layout introduces clarity and structure to the overall building frontage.

The side windows feature white prints of the complete city seal, while a designated glass panel serves for hand-lettering the daily menu.
